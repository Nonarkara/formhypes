## OpenTakeoff

The quantity schedule states how each area was measured, and it keeps a measured total beside an order total. That pairing is a pattern taken from [OpenTakeoff](https://github.com/Kentucky-ai/opentakeoff). FormHypes does not copy OpenTakeoff’s code. OpenTakeoff’s current licence is AGPL-3.0. The order total here equals the measured total, because this studio does not apply a waste or cutting allowance.
