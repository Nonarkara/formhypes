## What this pass changes

**A study has a name and a reason.** A bounded plain-text note asks who benefits, who bears a cost and what needs checking. It travels with saved and exported work. The system does not infer that a written intention has been met.

**Compare before choosing.** Pin the current design or choose Compare on a saved one. The reference is cloned and remains fixed during the session. A/B tower drawings use the same metre scale. Raw values and arithmetic deltas cover area, rentable share, total height, envelope, base-plate daylight proxy, annual contribution and illustrative capital cost. Feasibility and changed rent/regime/city/other scenario inputs are visible. Percent deltas are percentage points. No row is turned into a combined human score; amber marks change without implying improvement.

**A portable, validated study loop.** Import accepts versions3/4, checks state and nested inputs without changing the active Brief, rejects invalid/out-of-range data and ignores supplied computed metrics. Historical version3 files only gain the specifically absent later material/crown/garden/LED/detail defaults; invalid supplied values remain invalid. An import adds to the shelf and does not silently replace current work. Existing eight-study and storage-failure behavior remains. Version4 exports include names and notes.

Both options are recalculated by the current release; this is not an archival replay of an older engine. References last for the session and should be saved to survive reload. These scope limits are stated in the interface.

## Human needs still outside these numbers

Rentable area does not establish a good workplace. The base-plate proxy does not establish illumination, views or thermal comfort. Street context does not establish step-free access, safe crossings or usable public space. Shadow overlap is not an indoor daylight or rights-to-light assessment. Sampled reflected rays are not glare intensity. Wind loading is static sensitivity, not pedestrian airflow or structural approval.

Use these outputs to ask better questions of people and specialist analyses. Do not convert them into a citywide preference, cultural suitability or public-benefit score.

## A trial that could support a better-than claim

Recruit first-time architects and at least one occupant/community reviewer, with language and phone/desktop context recorded. Do not claim they are represented by agent personas. Give the same brief and task: preserve an initial scheme, alter an option, identify two consequences and one unknown, explain a provisional choice, export/reopen it, then recover the initial scheme. Where access/licensing allows, ask experienced users to perform the equivalent task in the other systems; a rendering task alone would unfairly favor their geometry strengths.

Record task completion, input/output interpretation errors, lost work, help requests and time. Ask which information affected the decision and which missing evidence would change it. A useful success criterion is correct recovery and explanation, not agreement with the optimizer. Revise when people misread the daylight proxy as indoor light, assume amber means good, or attribute a rent-input change to form. No participants were recruited or measured in this session; comparative human-centered superiority remains unverified.
