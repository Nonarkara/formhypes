## Mechanics experiment

Uniform fixed-base Euler–Bernoulli cantilever first-mode period: T = 2π/β1² × h² × sqrt(μ/EI), β1 = 1.8751040687. μ kg/m, EI N·m², h m, output seconds. The three structure names do not assign stiffness; users supply effective EI. Teaching example uses invented EI 60000/90000/120000 GN·m² and μ 400 tonnes/m. These are not measurements, design recommendations or calibration data. Soil-column T = 4H/Vs assumes one uniform layer over rigid rock; effective column Vs is not Vs30. All numerical fields begin empty. City changes clear soil inputs. Percentages report period changes, never suitability or safety.
